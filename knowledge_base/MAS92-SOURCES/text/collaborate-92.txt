 Collaboration sessions available in Maximo Manage
 The Collaborate Sessions application is now available in Maximo Manage as the Collab
 Session application under Work Orders.
 You can search, filter, and view collaboration sessions, including
 associated work orders, assets, and participants in the application.
 You can also download session summary PDFs after a session is completed,
 providing a centralized way to review collaboration activity across
 technicians and remote experts.
 For more information, see Collab Session application in Maximo Manage.
 Enhanced collaboration options for technicians in IBM Maximo Mobile
 Technicians can now collaborate with remote experts without starting a video or
 voice call by initiating a collaborate request by using Contact
 expert option in Collaborate application
 or Get assistance option in a Work order. Technicians can
 choose how a collaboration request is handled, either through a video or audio
 session or as a chat‑only interaction.These options are now available across
 Maximo Mobile, giving users easier
 access to real‑time support. When a remote expert accepts a collaboration
 request, they can immediately see whether the session is video, audio, or
 chat only, ensuring a smoother and more efficient hand-off.
