Upgrading

You can upgrade IBM® Maximo® Application Suite to keep up to date with new versions and updates to applications. You can also upgrade from IBM Maximo Asset Management to IBM Maximo Manage and from TRIRIGA to IBM Maximo Real Estate and Facilities.

  • Upgrading IBM Maximo Application Suite versions
    To keep IBM Maximo Application Suite up to date, you can upgrade to the latest version of Maximo Application Suite by subscribing to a channel. Before you upgrade, you must complete several prerequisite tasks to ensure that your Maximo Application Suite environment meets the upgrade requirements.
  • Upgrading from Maximo Asset Management to IBM Maximo Manage
    If you have Maximo Asset Management installed, you can upgrade to Maximo Manage in Maximo Application Suite. Previously known as IBM Maximo Asset Management, Maximo Manage is part of IBM Maximo Application Suite and has enhanced and new features.
  • Evolve your IBM Maximo Asset Management with the IBM Maximo Application Suite
    Technicians are scarce and unnecessary maintenance is costly. Achieving the next level of maintenance, productivity, and asset performance, requires a more intelligent and data-driven asset management approach that integrates teams, systems, processes, and runs anywhere.
  • Upgrading from TRIRIGA to Maximo Real Estate and Facilities
    If you have TRIRIGA installed, you can upgrade to Maximo Real Estate and Facilities in Maximo Application Suite. A high-level overview of the steps that are needed to migrate IBM TRIRIGA® or IBM TRIRIGA Application Suite to IBM Maximo Real Estate and Facilities Platform 9.1, including the TRIRIGA users and licenses. IBM Maximo Real Estate and Facilities is now an application in the Maximo Application Suite.
