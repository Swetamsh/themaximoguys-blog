# What Maximo Assistant and the Maximo AI Service Can Actually Do: The October 2026 Capability Reference

> 🎯 **Who this is for:** Maximo architects, administrators, reliability and supply-chain leads, and anyone who has been asked "can the Assistant do X?" and wants an answer they can defend. Every row below has an evidence tier, a version and a deployment model, and every capability has a way to check it in your own instance.

**Series:** Part 8 of 8 — *Maximo Assistant on MAS 9* | **Read time:** 22 minutes | **Series Finale** | **Last verified:** 20 October 2026

---

## 📌 Why This Post Exists

When this series started in July 2026, the Maximo Assistant was something you asked questions of. Three months later it is an agent that plans steps, calls tools and writes records, and it sits behind an MCP server that your own agents can also use. In between, the model changed, the set of objects it can read went from a fixed list to "any object you enable", and two security bulletins arrived for the service that runs it.

That speed causes a practical problem. Most claims about the Assistant you will hear in a steering meeting are either out of date, describe the Feature Channel as if it were production, or confuse it with a different product that has an almost identical name. This post is the corrective. It is a **dated reference**: what IBM has documented, what IBM has only announced, and what you have to build yourself, as of **20 October 2026**.

Three rules apply throughout:

- **Documented** means it is in IBM's what's-new or product documentation pages (we read cached copies of them).
- **Announced** means IBM has said it publicly (an announcement, an IBM Think article, a conference deck) but it is not in the product documentation we could read, or IBM itself used the future tense.
- **Not available — build it** means IBM has published nothing to support it, and the only way to get it is to build it with the 9.2 extension points.

We do not go back over the MAS 9.2 AI release in depth here. That is covered in [The AI Layer in MAS 9.2](/blog/mas-92-ai-features), which explains why the MCP server matters, how the confidence score works and what IBM did and did not commit to. This post takes the long view, from 9.0 to today, and adds what has happened since that post was published on 12 August.

## 🏷️ Two Products, One Confusing Name

Before any capability table, we have to fix a naming error, including one this series made.

**Maximo Assist** was the original MAS 8.x add-on for technician assistance: remote experts, guided help and knowledge capture. In MAS 9.x it lives on as **Maximo Collaborate**. Collaborate still ships on its own cadence; the 9.1 Collaborate component reached 9.1.15 on 29 September 2026. MAS 9.2 changes its packaging. IBM's 9.2 what's-new says: *"Collaborate is deprecated as an add-on in Maximo Application Suite. Instead, it is now an add-on in Maximo Manage."* Under the Collaborate 9.2 notes, the Expert Groups and Technicians applications move to Person Groups and Labor, and Manage 9.2 gets a **Collab Session** application under Work Orders, where you can review sessions and download session-summary PDFs.

**Maximo Assistant** is the watsonx-powered generative assistant. It first appeared in the Manage 9.1 what's-new as the "AI assistant", is configured in the **AI configuration** application, and requires the **Maximo AI Service**. Since 9.2 it is the agentic assistant this post is about.

| | Maximo Assist → Collaborate | Maximo Assistant |
|---|---|---|
| **Origin** | MAS 8.x add-on | Manage 9.1 ("AI assistant") |
| **What it does** | Remote-expert sessions, technician assistance, collaboration records | Generative and, since 9.2, agentic Q&A and actions over Maximo data |
| **Engine** | Collaboration and session tooling | Maximo AI Service + watsonx.ai models |
| **9.2 packaging** | Deprecated as a MAS add-on; becomes a **Manage add-on** | Enabled through the AI configuration app; metered in AppPoints |
| **Where users meet it** | Collab Session app (Work Orders), "Get assistance" from a work order | Header icon in Manage, Health, Operational Dashboard and asset dashboards; Maximo Mobile (online) |

<aside>

💡 **Correction to earlier parts:** The July 2026 versions of this series described Assist and Assistant as "the same family of capability described two ways". That was wrong, and the remote-expert and SME video material in Part 3 belongs to Collaborate (ex-Assist), not to the Assistant. Both posts were corrected in October 2026. If you are scoping a project, the test is simple. If it involves a live human expert on a video session, it is Collaborate. If it involves a model answering or acting, it is the Assistant.

</aside>

There is a second, smaller correction. Part 6 described AI access as licensed "per authorized user". IBM's own licensing text for the AI Service describes something else: usage is *"measured by the number of content tokens consumed by features enabled by the AI Service, such as the Maximo AI assistant"*, converted to AppPoints at **1 billion content tokens per month = 10 AppPoints**, and the AI Service install carries a 10 AppPoint allocation. Your contract may package this differently, so check it. But the metering unit IBM publishes is tokens, not seats.

## 🗓️ The Evolution: 9.0 to October 2026

Here is how the capability built up, in the order IBM shipped it. The "SaaS vs customer-managed" split matters at every row after 9.1. For customer-managed MAS, the Feature Channel is **non-production only**. For MAS as a Service, Feature Channel content can be used in production. IBM's June 2026 Feature Channel entry confirms that everything previewed before June 2026 became production-available at 9.2 GA.

| When | Release | What changed for AI | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2024 | **MAS 9.0** | Work Order Intelligence suggests problem codes from descriptions; AI features run through the **AI broker** | Documented |
| May 2025 | MAS SaaS | Maximo AI Service available in MAS SaaS **Standard** and **Premium** editions | Documented |
| 24 Jun 2025 | **Manage 9.1** | **AI assistant** answers questions in Manage, Health, Operational Dashboard and asset dashboards; **similar work orders**; **similar records**; **AI-recommended field values** on work orders; all require the **AI Service** | Documented |
| 1 Aug 2025 | AI Service 9.1 | AI Service **replaces the AI broker**; broker users must uninstall it and deploy AI Service 9.1 (also supported on MAS 9.0, with only 9.0 features) | Documented |
| 25 Nov 2025 | Model lifecycle | **IBM Granite 3.2 8b Instruct deprecated** | Documented |
| Dec 2025 | Feature Channel | **Header icon** launches the AI assistant; **AI-generated insights** on the Health Asset dashboard | Documented |
| Jan 2026 | Feature Channel | **gpt-oss-120b** usable for the `mcc`, `pcc`, `fmea` and `nl2oslc` templates (`nl2oslc` is the assistant) | Documented |
| Apr 2026 | Feature Channel | **"AI assistant supports all objects"**, including child and custom objects; the agentic option may appear but is *"not usable in the feature channel"* | Documented |
| 25 Jun 2026 | **MAS 9.2 GA** | **Agentic Assistant**, **Maximo MCP server** + custom tools, asset and alert **insights** with confidence, **Tickets** with similar tickets, **Mobile** assistant (online only), **redesigned AI configuration** + AppPoint tracking, **REF lease abstraction**, **Maximo IT Orchestrate agent** | Documented |
| 30 Jul / 27 Aug 2026 | AI Service 9.2.1 / 9.2.2 | Point releases on the monthly catalog train | Documented (release info) |
| 12 Aug 2026 | IBM Think | **Supervisor ALM agent** and four tools described; automation scripts as custom tools announced in the future tense | Announced |
| 29 Sep 2026 | **AI Service 9.2.3** | Ships with **Manage 9.2.4**; fix level for CVE-2026-15687 | Documented |

Two points in that table are worth stressing.

**The April 2026 change matters more than it looks.** Before it, the Assistant's data reach was a fixed set of objects. Afterwards, IBM documents that it *"can now find data for any Maximo Manage object and all child objects, including custom objects."* The default set is **assets, service requests, work orders, asset meters and other meters, preventive maintenance, work logs, assignments, job plans, and persons**, and *"you can enable it to find data for other objects."* Notice that inventory, items, purchase requisitions and purchase orders are not in the default list. A supply-chain user who opens the Assistant on an out-of-the-box 9.2 instance will not get answers about stock until an administrator enables those objects. That is a configuration task, not a product gap, but it explains many "it doesn't know about inventory" complaints.

**The model change happened on two dates.** SaaS customers could run gpt-oss-120b in production from January 2026. Customer-managed customers got it at 9.2 GA on 25 June 2026. If your compliance team asks when your Maximo started using a non-IBM model, the honest answer depends on your deployment model.

## 🤖 What 9.2 Added, in One Place

[The AI Layer in MAS 9.2](/blog/mas-92-ai-features) covers each 9.2 item in depth. Here is the summary, updated with the August Think article.

**The agentic Assistant.** IBM's 9.2 what's-new: the Assistant *"can now act as a single agent that analyzes your requests and works across a defined set of AI tools to answer them."* IBM's sample prompts are *"What assets need my attention?"*, *"How is asset [number] doing?"* and *"How do I do [task] in Maximo Manage?"*. IBM's 12 August 2026 Think article (Amy Huang, Product Manager, MAS AI) names the orchestrator: the **Supervisor ALM agent** *"is the head of the operation, and it's directly responsible for responding to requests."* Its job is to understand intent, break the request into logical steps and complete them with the capabilities available to the Assistant. The article lists four capabilities: **general knowledge search**, **IBM product guidance**, **data retrieval** and **condition insights**.

**Write actions.** The same article says users can *"take AI-assisted actions (create, update, modify) through simple natural language commands"*. The only examples IBM gives are **changing work order status**, **creating follow-up work orders** and **creating a service request**. These go through the Maximo MCP server.

**The Maximo MCP server.** The 9.2 what's-new: *"Create custom AI tools by using Maximo Manage automation scripts, object structures, or workflows, and connect your own tools and agents to the Maximo MCP server."* IBM's developer documentation (summarized in our DOC16 field guide) describes it as an `<instance>-mcp` deployment on OpenShift. Tools are generated from Manage: the AI configurations (`mcc`, `pcc`, `similarity`, `nl2oslc`, `docsearch`, `insightsgenerator`, allow-listed in the `MAXAICFGTOOL` table), plus custom tools from object structures, automation scripts, workflows and API routes. Every tool is a Maximo REST API POST, and IBM notes that *"Not all Maximo APIs are tools."* External agents connect through an exposed route with a Maximo API key or a token. ansible-devops 37.x added an MCP route to the Manage DNS entries in October 2026.

**Automation scripts as tools: one wording problem.** The 9.2 what's-new lists automation scripts among the things you can build custom tools from. The Think article, published seven weeks later, says *"You will also be able to use any of your automation scripts as custom tools."* The future tense suggests the general "any script as a tool" experience is still coming. We classify scripted custom tools on the MCP server as **Documented** and the broader "any automation script as an Assistant tool" experience as **Announced**. Watch TechXchange for which of the two IBM means.

**Insights with confidence.** Manage 9.2's alert insights analyze alert content, asset details, work history and meter data. *"Each insight includes a confidence score (high, medium, or low)"*, and you can view insights in the **Tickets** application and create pre-filled work orders from them. Asset insights cover asset condition, maintenance history and failure data.

**Tickets and similar tickets.** The new Tickets application handles all ticket classes in one list and includes **"Search for similar tickets"**. Alongside the 9.1 similar work orders and similar records, this is the third documented similarity surface.

**Mobile.** From the Mobile 9.2 what's-new: *"If you have AI assistant configured for Maximo Manage and security permissions to access it, you can use AI assistant in Maximo Mobile. Currently, it works only in online mode."*

**Redesigned AI configuration and AppPoint tracking.** The app shows model status, enables capabilities with one click and links to AppPoint usage dashboards. **Actions > Check AppPoints** opens the licensing dashboards, where AI Service appears in the Instances table. This replaces a real 9.1 gap: IBM's 9.1 documentation stated *"You cannot track AppPoint usage for Maximo AI Service in Maximo Application Suite."*

**Beyond Manage.** In Real Estate and Facilities 9.2, **AI lease abstraction** takes up to **5 documents per lease, 15 MB each, PDF or DOCX**, requires the AI Service, and shows the source location of each field so a person can review it. **Maximo IT 9.2** runs a **watsonx Orchestrate** agent in the background that summarizes incidents and drafts a solution record once an incident is Resolved. Service agents review and publish that record.

## 🧬 The Model Bill of Materials

"Which model is it?" now has a different answer depending on which feature you mean. This is the bill of materials as IBM documents it, followed by what to confirm for your own deployment.

| Component | Model / template | Used for | Status (Oct 2026) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Generative LLM | **gpt-oss-120b** | Most AI Service features; `mcc`, `pcc`, `fmea`, `nl2oslc` templates | Current; on SaaS since Jan 2026, customer-managed since 9.2 GA | Feature Channel what's-new (Jan 2026); IBM on-prem watsonx doc |
| Assistant template | **`nl2oslc`** | Natural language → OSLC API calls (the AI assistant) | Current | Feature Channel what's-new |
| Agentic Assistant templates | `nl2oslc`, `insightsgenerator`, `docsearch` | Agentic Assistant tool chain | Current (9.2) | IBM AI Service feature table (via DOC14) |
| Alternative LLM | **Llama-4-Maverick-17B-128E-Instruct-FP8** | Listed as a model option for the agentic Assistant | Option | IBM AI Service material (via DOC14) |
| Embeddings | **`embedding_transformer_en_slate.125m`** (Watson NLP) | Similarity: similar WOs, similar records, similar tickets | Current, on-prem template | IBM on-prem watsonx doc (via DOC14) |
| Classic ML | MCC / PCC models | Trained locally in the AI Service on OpenShift | Current | IBM AI Service data-privacy topic |
| Legacy LLM | **IBM Granite 3.2 8b Instruct** | Former default for several templates | **Deprecated 25 Nov 2025** | Feature Channel what's-new |

Four operational facts sit next to the table:

1. **English only.** IBM states that AI Service inferencing and training support only English. Data that is not in English cannot be processed or used to generate output. For multilingual operations, this is a scoping constraint, not a detail.
2. **Inference is stateless at watsonx.ai.** IBM's data-privacy topic says that when used with the AI Service, watsonx.ai does not retain inference data or results, exchanges are encrypted with TLS 1.2, and MCC/PCC training happens locally in OpenShift.
3. **One tenant ID per environment.** The AI Service has to be enabled separately in production, development and test, each with its own tenant ID.
4. **Bundled watsonx.ai is limited-use.** The AI Service includes a limited-use watsonx.ai license for Maximo AI features. watsonx.governance, watsonx Orchestrate (needed for the Maximo IT agent) and watsonx.data are licensed separately.

<aside>

💡 **Key insight:** "Granite was replaced" is the version of this story you will hear, and it is imprecise. IBM's wording was *"You can now use the gpt-oss-120b model"*, which describes an option with a documented migration path, while Granite 3.2 8b Instruct is deprecated. If you deployed before 2026 and never ran the migration, your instance may still be on the deprecated model. Check it.

</aside>

|---|---|---|---|

What to do: **run AI Service 9.1.19 or 9.2.3 or later.** With a 9.8 code-injection CVE and no workaround, this is an upgrade-now item, not a next-maintenance-window item. Since 9.2, the AI Service is also the component that hosts what your agents can do. It sits behind the MCP server, which a growing set of external agents can call. Patch it like an internet-facing integration tier, not like a reporting add-on. Our security series covers the hardening side in [Securing MAS for AI Agents and MCP](/blog/mas-secops-securing-ai-agents-mcp).

<aside>

💡 **Key insight:** The pattern to notice is that **IBM published the low-severity bulletin and the trackers published the critical one**. Do not wait for an IBM bulletin to match a 9.8 CVSS before you patch, because the fixed version the trackers cite is one you should be on anyway. Do not cite a tracker as IBM either. When you report to your security team, say exactly which source said what.

## 🚧 What It Cannot Do Yet

These are the capability requests we hear most often that **IBM has not documented**. For each one we also say whether the 9.2 extension points make it buildable. The answer is usually yes, with a human in the loop.

| Request | Native today? | Why not | Buildable on 9.2? |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Create an item for this part" | **No** | No IBM-documented Assistant tool or example creates item master records | Yes: custom MCP tool on an item object structure; land the item in **PENDING**, promote to ACTIVE through governance |
| "Raise a PR for these parts" | **No** | IBM's write examples stop at WO status, follow-up WO and SR | Yes: custom tool + automation script assembles the PR; land in **WAPPR**; route through your PR approval workflow; never auto-approve |
| "Is this item a duplicate?" | **No** | Similarity is documented for work orders, records and tickets, **not the item master** | Yes, as a TMG design: your own embedding store (e.g. watsonx.ai slate embeddings) or deterministic matching on classification attributes and manufacturer/model number |
| "Issue 2 of item X to WO Y" (inventory usage) | **No** | Not IBM-documented, despite older third-party claims | Yes, as a custom tool, with the same confirmation rules |
| "Use any of my automation scripts as Assistant tools" | **Partly** | Scripts are a documented MCP tool source; the general "any script" experience is IBM's future tense | Yes via MCP today; watch for the native experience |
| Answer stock questions out of the box | **Not by default** | Inventory/item objects are not in the default object list | Yes: enable additional objects in the assistant configuration (April 2026 FC capability) |
| Work offline on Mobile | **No** | IBM: *"Currently, it works only in online mode"* | No: wait for IBM |
| Process non-English data | **No** | AI Service supports English only | No |
| Maximo Condition Insight, AI in FSM, AI incident classification | **Announced only** | Not in the product documentation we read | Wait for documentation |

The supply-chain rows have their own post. [Part 7 — Supply Chain with the Assistant: Items, PRs and Similarity](/blog/mas-assist-supply-chain-items-prs-similarity) turns the "buildable" column into a design: which object structures, which automation scripts, where the human confirmation sits, and how to do duplicate detection without pretending IBM ships it. If you read one post after this one, read that.

<aside>

💡 **Key insight:** "Not native" does not mean "not possible". It means **the governance is yours**. When IBM builds a write action, IBM decides how it validates. When you build one as an MCP tool, the tool inherits Manage's REST security, business rules and validation, but the scoping, the confirmation step and the audit trail are your design decisions. Start with read-only tools, use a least-privilege identity per tool set, and log every invocation.

</aside>

💡 **Key insight:** Run this table once per environment, not once per program. ## 🧭 Series Retrospective: What Changed Since Part 1

When Part 1 went live, the central theme of this series was "an assistant, not an agent". That was accurate for 9.1 and is no longer accurate for 9.2. The governance principle behind it still holds, and it now matters more: **the AI recommends, and a human decides, on every action that matters.** What changed is where that principle is enforced. In 9.1 it was enforced by the product's limits. In 9.2 it is enforced by **your** tool design, your security groups and your confirmation steps, because the product can now act.

Three habits from this series carry forward:

- **Tier every claim.** Documented, Announced or build-it. Put only the first tier on a roadmap with dates.
- **Name the deployment model.** "9.2" means different histories on SaaS and customer-managed, because the Feature Channel means different things on each.
- **Verify in your own instance.** Documentation tells you what the product can do. The checklist above tells you what your instance actually does.

## Key Takeaways

- **Maximo Assist ≠ Maximo Assistant.** Assist (8.x) became **Collaborate**, which is deprecated as a MAS add-on and becomes a **Manage add-on** in 9.2. **Assistant** is the watsonx generative assistant from Manage 9.1.
- **9.2 made the Assistant agentic.** A **Supervisor ALM agent** coordinates general knowledge search, IBM product guidance, data retrieval and condition insights. Its documented writes are WO status, follow-up WO and SR, through the **Maximo MCP server**.
- **Reach is now configurable.** Since the April 2026 Feature Channel the Assistant can read **any Manage object, including custom objects**, but inventory and items are not in the default list.
- **Know your models and your patch level.** gpt-oss-120b plus slate embeddings, with Granite 3.2 8b Instruct deprecated. **AI Service 9.2.3** (with Manage 9.2.4, 29 Sep 2026) is current and is the fix line for the October 2026 AI Service CVEs. Only CVE-2026-15687 is IBM-bulletin-confirmed.
- **Supply-chain writes are build-it.** Creating items, creating PRs and detecting duplicate items are not native. Build them as MCP custom tools with human confirmation, PENDING items and WAPPR PRs ([Part 7](/blog/mas-assist-supply-chain-items-prs-similarity)).

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### What can the Maximo Assistant do as of October 2026?

On MAS 9.2 with the Maximo AI Service, the Assistant acts as a single agent that analyzes your request and works across a defined set of AI tools. IBM's August 2026 description names four: general knowledge search, IBM product guidance, data retrieval and condition insights. It can find data in any Manage object and its child objects, including custom objects, that you enable. By default that covers assets, service requests, work orders, meters, PMs, work logs, assignments, job plans and persons. It can take AI-assisted write actions through the Maximo MCP server, and IBM's published examples are changing WO status, creating follow-up WOs and creating SRs. It is also available in Maximo Mobile, online only.

### Is Maximo Assist the same product as Maximo Assistant?

No. Maximo Assist was the MAS 8.x add-on for technician assistance and remote experts. It lives on as Maximo Collaborate, which MAS 9.2 deprecates as a suite add-on and makes an add-on in Maximo Manage, with a Collab Session application under Work Orders. Maximo Assistant is the generative assistant introduced in Manage 9.1, delivered by the AI Service and configured in the AI configuration app. Earlier parts of this series called them the same family. They are not.

### Which AI models does the Maximo AI Service use?

gpt-oss-120b powers most features and has been usable for the `mcc`, `pcc`, `fmea` and `nl2oslc` (assistant) templates since the January 2026 Feature Channel: in production on SaaS from January, and on customer-managed MAS from 9.2 GA. Similarity uses the `embedding_transformer_en_slate.125m` embedding model on-premises. IBM's AI Service material lists Llama-4-Maverick-17B-128E-Instruct-FP8 as an option for the agentic Assistant. IBM Granite 3.2 8b Instruct was deprecated on 25 November 2025. Confirm in the AI configuration app which model your instance actually uses.

### Can the Maximo Assistant create items or purchase requisitions?

Not natively. IBM has published nothing that covers creating items, creating PRs or detecting duplicate items. On 9.2 you can build them as custom tools on the Maximo MCP server using object structures, automation scripts and workflows. Keep a human confirmation step, create items in PENDING, create PRs in WAPPR, and never auto-approve. [Part 7](/blog/mas-assist-supply-chain-items-prs-similarity) is the design guide.

### Does the Maximo Assistant only work with watsonx, or can I use a third-party LLM and my own API keys?

**The built-in Assistant: watsonx only.** It runs through the Maximo AI Service, which sends model requests either to watsonx.ai or to a local runtime that ships with it. The models include non-IBM ones (OpenAI's open-weights gpt-oss-120b, a Llama-4-Maverick option), but they come from the watsonx catalogue, not from your own provider account. IBM documents no way to point the Assistant, its field recommendations or its similarity search at your own OpenAI, Azure OpenAI, Bedrock or Anthropic key. The only key in a SaaS or hybrid setup is the AI Service key IBM issues (`mxe.int.aibrokerapikey`), not an LLM provider key.

**Your own LLM: yes, from the outside.** On MAS 9.2, any agent that speaks MCP (Claude, ChatGPT or Copilot, Cursor, IBM Bob, your own agent) can connect to the Maximo MCP server with a Maximo API key or access token and call the tools you expose. The LLM and its provider key live in your agent; Maximo is the tool provider, and its security and business rules still apply to every call. Before 9.2, the same pattern works over the REST/OSLC APIs. So you cannot swap the model behind the built-in Assistant, but you can put your own AI in front of Maximo. That is the path [Part 7](/blog/mas-assist-supply-chain-items-prs-similarity) uses for its create-item and draft-PR tools.

### Which AI Service version should I be on, and what security issues affect it?

The current level is AI Service 9.2.3 (29 September 2026, alongside Manage 9.2.4). IBM's 1 October 2026 bulletin for CVE-2026-15687 (CVSS 2.4) lists 9.1.0 and 9.2.0 as affected and 9.1.19 or 9.2.3 as fixed. IBM's 5 October 2026 bulletin covers the critical CVE-2026-90920 (CVSS 9.8, code injection) and bundled-component CVEs with the same fixes and no workaround. Be on 9.1.19 or 9.2.3 or later.
