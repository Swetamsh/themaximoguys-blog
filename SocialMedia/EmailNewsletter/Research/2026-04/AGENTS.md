## Folder Purpose

This directory contains completed research artifacts for the April 2026 email newsletter workstream. The lowercase `claude.md` file in this folder is a research briefing, not repository-level operating instructions.

## Working Rules

- Treat existing research files as source artifacts. Preserve citations, dates, verification notes, and uncertainty flags.
- Do not rewrite historical research unless the task explicitly asks for an update or correction.
- When adding new research, include the research date, source URLs, and any verification caveats.
- Follow the parent repository guidance in `/root/themaximoguys-blog/AGENTS.md` for blog, Sanity, social media, and publishing workflows.
